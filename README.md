# NestLink

**A desk lamp that glows with how your loved one is doing.**

NestLink is a product design concept for families who live apart. Everyday
objects at an older adult's home (a cup, a door handle, a sound artifact)
sense quietly in the background. A lamp on the family member's desk turns
that into warm, glanceable light. There is no screen, camera or wearable, so
reassurance never feels like surveillance.

🔗 **Live site:** https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/nestlink/

## About the project

This site walks through the design process, from research to product concept:

- Research: brainstorming, interviews, AEIOU framework, personas and empathy maps
- Problem and "How might we": care at a distance without surveillance or dependency
- Ideation: sketches and form exploration
- Solution: the sensing objects and the desk lamp, including the lamp's states
- Prototype: electronics and form models
- Future scope: NestLink as a home-products ecosystem

Created at the National Institute of Design (Problem Solving course)
by Preksha Anand.
