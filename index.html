03 / Background and context

India has over 140 million citizens above the age of 60.

Nuclear family structures and migration for work mean many elderly parents live away from their adult children. Existing solutions often depend on cameras, trackers, or behavior change from the parent, creating friction where dignity and trust are essential.

Aging in place Remote caregiving Privacy sensitivity Low cognitive load
04 / Interview findings

Caregivers wanted awareness without becoming surveillance operators.

Interview synthesis pointed to anxiety, distance, reluctance to ask for help, and the emotional weight of constant checking. The clearest opportunity was a system that stays calm until something matters.

Insight 01

Safety cannot feel like control.

Insight 02

Parents resist tools that mark them as dependent.

Insight 03

Adult children need fewer check-ins, not more dashboards.

Insight 04

Emergency signals need priority over routine data.

05 / Final problem context

Millions of distant caregivers in India worry about their aging parents every single day — but every tool built to help them either surveils the parent or gets abandoned within weeks.

Cameras feel like control. Wearables get forgotten. More calls burden the very person you're trying to protect.

So parents say "sab theek hai" — even when it isn't.

The distant caregiver stays anxious. The parent stays silent. The gap grows.

06 / How might we

How might we enable adult children to stay meaningfully aware of their aging parents' well-being, without introducing surveillance, dependency, or behavioral change for the parents?

08 / Solution ideation
09 / Final solution

NestLink transforms everyday household objects into a discreet care network.

It replaces wearables and cameras with passive sensing objects that communicate through a screenless ambient receiver.

10 / Forms and product system

Smart Cup

Captures biometric snapshots through natural touch during hydration.

Smart Door Knob

Detects movement patterns through routine door interactions.

Sound Artifact

Recognizes abnormal sounds or distress keywords on threshold events.

Central Receiver

Aggregates signals and expresses priority through light and sound.

11 / State Diagram
12 / Features
Passive monitoring

Data is collected through ordinary interactions, not explicit check-ins.

No wearables

The elderly user does not need to charge, wear, or remember a device.

Ambient feedback

Light patterns communicate system state with low cognitive load.

Priority alerts

Critical health and emergency events override routine signals.
